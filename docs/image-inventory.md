# Medixo AI imagery refresh

## Hero gallery

The first eight portraits follow the canonical service catalogue. The last four show industry settings without adding services or implying client work. All twelve are cropped to 3:4 and served as local WebPs with a 288 × 384 option. The Summit portrait is cropped from the [DOE publication](https://www.energy.gov/articles/oak-ridge-national-laboratory-launches-americas-new-top-supercomputer-science) of Carlos Jones’s photograph for Oak Ridge National Laboratory, licensed under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). The remaining eleven are native portraits under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The primary files are 480 × 640 except for Summit (570 × 760), optical inspection (420 × 560), and robotic welding (456 × 608). The crops have no colour treatment. The industry directory and portfolio have separate imagery.

This repository has the same owner as Medixo's site but no declared reusable license. **Confirm the project rights holder approves public use of these screenshots before publishing.** The stock photo licenses do not grant rights to the product UI.
- **EduX:** local checkout of [`edux` at `11a8a70`](https://github.com/AJBiswojit/edux/tree/11a8a7009e0b12929dd9d1254dc58fbf8d6eed5c), with MIT notice included.
